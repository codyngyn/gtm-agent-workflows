You enter judgment calls. The Scoring tab formula does the math. Cody overrides any rating he disagrees with.

## Factors (0 to 5 each)

| Factor | Weight | What 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Hiring proximity | 30% | Owns or directly influences hiring for the role |
| Path similarity | 20% | Made a career move like Cody's |
| Alumni tie | 15% | Same school |
| Warm path | 15% | Referral, intro or prior relationship |
| Activity and reachability | 10% | Posts often; reachable on the allowed channels |
| Recent trigger | 10% | A new role, a hiring post or a talk in the last 60 days |

## Rules

- Generic titles ("GTM") don't count as evidence of hiring proximity. Check the profile.
- Event contacts and target-company alums can't hire. Rate their hiring proximity 0 to 2: 2 only when the source shows they can refer into a target company or advise its hiring team.
- Active interview contacts are part of a live hiring process, so the event-contact cap does not apply. Rate hiring proximity by their role in it: 5 for the hiring manager, 4 for the recruiter or interviewer running the process.
- A scheduled in-person meeting at an event counts as a warm path of 2 to 3. It is not a referral. The event counts as a recent trigger when it is within 60 days.
- Write a short reason for each rating in Score reasons.
- Suggest the day's sends: at most two new cold first touches per company. Cody makes the final pick, and he may override the ranking (for example, peers before leaders).
